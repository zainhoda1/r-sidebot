This plot completely reverses the earlier conclusion! **Within every species, larger wingspans are associated with faster flight** — all three trend lines slope upward. The negative relationship we saw before was a classic case of **Simpson's Paradox**: the species happen to differ in both typical wingspan and typical speed (Forest Dragons are smaller and faster overall; Sea Dragons are larger but slower), which created a misleading negative trend when the data were pooled.

So the breeder's answer depends on their strategy:
- **Within a species**, they should breed for **larger wingspans** to get faster dragons.
- However, if they're open to choosing a species, **Forest Dragons** appear to be the fastest overall despite their smaller size, so breeding Forest Dragons with larger wingspans would likely be the best approach.
