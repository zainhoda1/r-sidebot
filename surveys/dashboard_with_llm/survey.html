Coffee Sourcing Dashboard Survey

Welcome! You are a buyer for a coffee importer. The dashboard shows data on 600 coffee farms: the altitude the farm sits at, its size in hectares, the average age of its trees, how many kilograms it yields per hectare, and the price per kilogram its coffee sold for at auction. Each farm grows one of five arabica varietals.

Use the dashboard to explore the data, then answer the questions that follow.
